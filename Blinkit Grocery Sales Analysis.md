Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis 

Tools Used: Power BI • Power Query • DAX Measures 

1\. Project Objective 

To analyze grocery sales data and understand sales performance across product categories, fat content, outlet sizes, outlet types, and establishment years. 

2\. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) 

The dashboard includes four main performance indicators to track commercial volume and customer satisfaction: 

| KPI Metric  | Analytical Focus |
| :---- | :---- |
| Total Sales  | Aggregate revenue generated across selected product categories and retail outlets. |
| Number of Items  | Total count of distinct or transacted grocery items within the filtered scope. |
| Average Sales  | Mean revenue contribution per item or transaction across segments. |
| Average Rating  | Mean customer rating score used to evaluate product reception alongside revenue. |

3\. Analysis Approach 

1\. Import and explore the Blinkit grocery dataset. 

2\. Organize sales and outlet-related fields. 

3\. Create measures for key business KPIs. 

4\. Build interactive charts and a metric-selection slicer. 

5\. Compare sales across product categories and outlet characteristics. 

4\. Dashboard Insights 

The dashboard is designed to examine six core dimensions of grocery and outlet performance: 

| Analysis Dimension  | Dashboard Examination Scope |
| :---- | :---- |
| Fat Content  | Sales distribution by item fat content. |
| Item Type  | Sales comparison across grocery product categories. |
| Outlet Location  | Sales comparison across different location types. |
| Outlet Establishment  | Sales across outlet establishment years. |

Identify product categories contributing strongly to sales. 

Compare outlet performance to understand location-based opportunities.

Use customer ratings alongside sales to evaluate product performance. 

Disclaimer & Analytical Note 

This project is created for educational and portfolio purposes. The analysis is based on the available dataset, and findings should not be treated as guaranteed business outcomes.
